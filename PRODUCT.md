# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

New and returning Hapi Matcha Club customers, primarily browsing on mobile while deciding what to order. Hapi staff also need the public menu and brand presentation to be easy to keep accurate as products and availability change.

## Product Purpose

Hapi Matcha Club Digital Menu is a premium public website that makes customers crave a product, choose confidently, and reach an official ordering channel quickly. It is a menu and brand experience, not a custom checkout or delivery system.

## Brand Personality

Playful, cozy, premium. The experience should feel youthful and warm without becoming childish, and Japanese-inspired without relying on stereotypes.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, generic restaurant templates, excessive gradients, glassmorphism-heavy styling, oversized text that overwhelms the menu, stock photography, and animation that competes with the food.

## Design Principles

- Put the menu and product photography at the center of the experience.
- Make prices, sizes, availability, and ordering paths easy to scan.
- Use distinctive brand-led details instead of decorative UI patterns.
- Keep the path from discovery to ordering short and obvious.
- Treat mobile as the primary design surface, then scale the system up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target Lighthouse Accessibility 95+. Use semantic HTML, logical heading order, descriptive image alternatives, strong contrast, visible focus states, comfortable touch targets, keyboard-accessible controls, and reduced-motion alternatives.
